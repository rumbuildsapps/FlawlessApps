# FlawlessApps
Flawless Apps is an AI business growth platform featuring Finesse, a conversational advisor, business assessments, appointment scheduling, and tools for automating client workflows. Because “we’ve always done it this way” isn’t a growth strategy.

**Meet Finesse**
Finesse is our AI business growth advisor: helping visitors explore services, identify opportunities, and take the next step—with a little personality.

**Planned Features**
- AI chatbot for service guidance and business growth questions.
- Business assessments to understand each client’s goals and challenges.
- Appointment booking, rescheduling, and reminders.
- Email, calendar, and WhatsApp integrations with user consent.
- Client workspaces for projects, deliverables, and reporting.
- Admin tools for leads, clients, services, and subscriptions.
  
**Vision**
Bring strategy, social media growth, and workflow automation into one connected platform that helps business owners spend less time on repetitive work and more time building their business.
Project Status
In development. This README describes the product vision; feature availability will evolve as implementation progresses.
